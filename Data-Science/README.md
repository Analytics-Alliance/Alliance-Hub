# Data Science

Welcome to the **Data Science** section of Alliance Hub! 📊  
This section is a space to explore tools, techniques, and projects related to analyzing, visualizing, and modeling data.

---

## **What You’ll Find Here**

- **Notebooks/**: Jupyter Notebooks for data analysis, visualization, and machine learning.  
- **Notes/**: Step-by-step guides, theoretical write-ups, and helpful explanations.  
- **Case-Studies/**: Real-world applications and practical examples.

---

## **How You Can Contribute**

Your contributions can help this section grow! Here’s how to participate:  
1. Browse the content already available to see what inspires you.  
2. Add your contributions to the relevant folder (e.g., `Notebooks/`, `Notes/`).  
3. Follow the [contribution guidelines](../CONTRIBUTING.md) to submit a pull request.

Contributions can include:
- Jupyter Notebooks for practical data science workflows.  
- Notes on data science concepts or methodologies.  
- Case studies showcasing data science in action.
