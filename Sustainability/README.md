# Sustainability

Welcome to the **Sustainability** section of Alliance Hub! 🌱  
This folder is dedicated to sharing knowledge, tools, and ideas to inspire action toward a more sustainable future.

---

## **What You’ll Find Here**

- **Notebooks/**: Data-driven projects and analyses related to sustainability.  
- **Notes/**: Frameworks, research summaries, and guides.  
- **Case-Studies/**: Real-world examples of sustainability in action.

---

## **How You Can Contribute**

Your ideas and insights are invaluable! Here’s how to contribute:  
1. Browse the folder to find inspiration or gaps to fill.  
2. Add your work to the appropriate folder (e.g., `Notebooks/`, `Notes/`, or `Case-Studies/`).  
3. Follow the [contribution guidelines](../CONTRIBUTING.md) to submit a pull request.

Contributions can include:
- Jupyter Notebooks analyzing sustainability challenges.  
- Notes on frameworks or sustainable practices.  
- Case studies showcasing successful implementations.
