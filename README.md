# Alliance Hub

Welcome to the Alliance Hub! This repository is a collaborative space where members of the Analytics Alliance contribute resources, tutorials, and knowledge across various disciplines, including Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Sustainability, and more.

Our mission is to foster learning, collaboration, and innovation by providing high-quality, structured resources.

---

## Repository Structure

The repository is organized into the following sections:

- [Artificial Intelligence](./Artificial_Intelligence): Explore notebooks, notes, and tutorials related to AI concepts like machine learning and neural networks.
- [Data Science](./Data_Science): Resources for data analysis, modeling, and visualization.
- [Computer Science](./Computer_Science): Notes and tutorials on programming, algorithms, and systems.
- [Sustainability](./Sustainability): Content focused on tackling global challenges through innovative solutions.
- [Mind Maps](./Mind_Maps): Visual representations of workflows, ideas, and concepts.
- [Shared Resources](./Shared_Resources): Templates, datasets, and other reusable tools.
- [Tutorials](./Tutorials): Beginner-friendly guides to help you get started.

---

## How to Contribute

We welcome contributions from members! To contribute:
1. Review the folder structure and identify the best location for your content.
2. Follow the contribution guidelines in [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
3. Submit a pull request with a brief description of your contribution.
