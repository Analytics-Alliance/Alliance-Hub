## **How You Can Contribute**

We’re excited to see your tutorials! Here’s how to contribute:  

1. **Choose the Right Format**:  
   - **Interactive Tutorials**: Use `.ipynb` (Jupyter Notebook).  
   - **Text-Based Guides**: Use `.md` (Markdown) or `.pdf` (PDF).  

2. **Create Your Tutorial**:  
   - Include a clear title, description, and step-by-step instructions.  
   - Add visuals like diagrams, screenshots, or examples where possible.  

3. **Use a Descriptive Filename**:  
   - Examples:  
     - `git_basics_tutorial.ipynb`  
     - `python_setup_guide.pdf`  

4. **Submit Your Work**:  
   - Add your file to this folder.  
   - Include a brief explanation of your tutorial’s purpose in your pull request.
