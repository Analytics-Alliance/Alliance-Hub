# Artificial Intelligence

This folder is dedicated to resources related to Artificial Intelligence, including:

- **Notebooks**: Interactive Jupyter Notebooks for practical implementations and hands-on learning.
- **Notes**: Summaries, explanations, and concept overviews to deepen understanding of AI topics.

---

## Subfolders

- [Notebooks](./Notebooks): Dive into practical examples and implementations of AI concepts.
- [Notes](./Notes): Explore written resources, concept explanations, and study guides.

---

## Contribution Guidelines

We welcome your contributions! Here's how to contribute to this folder:
1. Add Jupyter Notebooks to the `Notebooks` subfolder. Ensure they are clean, well-documented, and functional.
2. Add any text-based notes or documentation to the `Notes` subfolder.
3. Use clear, descriptive filenames (e.g., `neural_network_basics.ipynb` or `ai_intro_notes.pdf`).
4. Include references or links if applicable.

Together, let's make AI accessible and comprehensive for everyone!
