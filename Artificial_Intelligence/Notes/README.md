# Notes

This folder contains written resources such as summaries, explanations, and guides. The content in these notes provides theoretical and conceptual knowledge for a wide range of topics.

---

## Purpose

The notes in this folder aim to:
- Offer detailed explanations and overviews of key concepts and techniques.
- Complement practical resources by providing theoretical context.
- Act as study aids for learners exploring various disciplines.

---

## File Guidelines

When adding notes:
1. **File Format**: Use `.md` (Markdown) or other readable text formats (e.g., `.pdf`, `.txt`).
2. **Naming**: Use descriptive filenames that reflect the content (e.g., `introduction_to_statistics.md` or `machine_learning_concepts.pdf`).
3. **Structure**: Ensure your notes are well-organized with:
   - A title and summary at the top.
   - Section headers for different topics or subtopics.
   - Clear and concise language with bullet points or numbered lists as needed.

---

## Contribution Guidelines

1. Add your notes to the relevant topic folder (e.g., AI, Data Science, etc.).
2. Include references or citations if your notes are based on external resources.
3. Test for readability and clarity to ensure they are accessible to others.

Thank you for contributing to the shared knowledge base!
